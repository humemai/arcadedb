How the store is used:
- Memory holds what was remembered: its content, its kind (event, preference, plan, note, summary), its importance (1 to 5) and the day it was created (text, YYYY-MM-DD).
- Person holds the people and Topic the topics. A memory is filed under topics (ABOUT) and mentions persons (MENTIONS).
- Fact holds one attribute of a person (city, employer, diet) with the days it holds from and to (valid_to is null while the fact is still valid). A fact describes a person (DESCRIBES); a newer fact supersedes the older one it replaces (SUPERSEDES, from the newer fact to the older).
- Every record has a numeric id, unique within its table. A new record gets the next free number: the largest id of its table plus one. When a task lists several new records, number them in the order listed.
- Change nothing that the task does not ask for.

You may make at most {budget} tool calls for the whole task. A task can have several sessions, and they share these calls.
